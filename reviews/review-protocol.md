# Internal mathematical review protocol

State exact hypotheses and source classification. An initially separate proof reviewer checks deductions; a counterexample reviewer tests boundary and failure cases; a generalization reviewer checks scope, weaker hypotheses and prior-result boundaries. Record actual agent names and supplied context; later discussion is allowed but distinguish it from initial independent analysis. Review mathematical proofs directly; agreement and finite computations do not prove universality. No AI review is external peer review or human author approval. Novelty needs primary-source comparison beyond a search returning no matches.

For each result: assumptions; direct derivation; attempted counterexamples; exact checks; zero/boundary cases; literature status; stronger/corrected formulations; proof simplification. Keep failed statements and corrected versions together. Candidate extensions stay in research/ until independently audited and source-compared.
