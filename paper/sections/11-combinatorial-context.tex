\section{Supporting combinatorial identifications}
The classical excursion--plane-tree--Dyck and dissection correspondences \cite{HacklEtAl,BrakEtAl,CurienKortchemski} retain complete profiles. If an excursion has $r_a$ steps $-a$, $u=\sum_a ar_a$ upsteps and length $N=u+\sum_a r_a$, its count is
\[
\frac{N!}{(u+1)!\prod_a r_a!}.
\]
Indeed, degree words have $u+1$ leaves and $r_a$ vertices of degree $a+1$; their increment sum is $-1$, so the cycle lemma divides their multinomial count by $N+1$. For a nonempty profile with $a\ge1$, the polygon has $u+2$ boundary sides, $\sum r_a-1$ diagonals and $r_a$ faces of size $a+2$. These are classical refinements, not principal new results.

The axis-extraction packed-word family in Section 23 of \cite{Ham2019} is resolved by the primary definition and formula of Novelli--Thibon \cite[Section 5.3, (182)]{NovelliThibon}. A packed word uses an initial interval of positive letters; it is $m$-packed when every multiplicity is divisible by $m$. Sylvester equivalence is generated by adjacent exchanges of $a,c$ in the presence of a later $b$ with $a\le b<c$. Its classes at length $mn$ have the same count as nonnegative paths with upstep $(1,1)$ and vertical downsteps $(0,-mk)$ to $(mn,0)$. Writing $F(z)=(1-z)/(1-2z)$, their formula is $[z^n]F(z)^{mn+1}/(mn+1)$. Lagrange inversion with $Z=tF(Z)^m$ gives $H=F(Z)=1+tH^m(2H-1)$, the independently derived path series. This proves equality of counts with exact indexing, but supplies no direct class bijection or blob interpretation.
