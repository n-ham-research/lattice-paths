\section{Research status and next questions}
The structural and trajectory questions have different answers. Weighted boundary graphs yield exact finite conditions and widening-funnel rigidity; finite-range actual trajectories encounter a separate mass-growth obstruction. Labelled endpoint rows permit reconstruction under explicit pivots. The main open converse concerns nonextinct infinite-support trajectories for $q>1$, beyond positivity and short finite prefixes.

The arguments are directly proved and internally reviewed, but historical priority and standalone publication value remain unresolved. A focused comparison with structured-matrix and killed-walk theory is required. General-$q$ cone rigidity, optimal trajectory horizons and direct packed-class bijections are natural next questions. The broader 150-claim audit is supporting material, not the manuscript's organizing principle. No submission or external collaboration is implied.
