\section{Research status and next questions}
Structural and actual regularity differ, but can be joined by inverse questions. Scalar actual uniformity identifies a constant kernel on widening funnels. Parity observations with equal initial weights identify a constant triangular kernel after a sharp finite seed. Finite-range boundary propagation instead supplies exclusion certificates for proposed support classes, while full labelled data admit reconstruction with explicit pivots.

These conceptual proofs and exact counterexamples are internally reviewed; their historical priority remains unresolved. The appropriate next step is specialist recognition of the short statements, followed by discussion of development. General-$q$ actual converses, unequal initial weights, optimal origin-start certificates, signed kernels and useful compressed-data equivalences remain open. Classical factorial/tree/polygon results and the broader 150-claim audit are preserved separately as supporting research. Neither submission readiness nor external collaboration is implied.
