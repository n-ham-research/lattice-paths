\section{Scope and further work}
The six step-set variants above are explained by two different layer principles. Exact computations through column twelve and explicit path generation through column five provide reproducibility checks; the proofs establish the identities for all indices. A complete source inventory and records of indexing corrections accompany this manuscript.

The remaining programme is to classify residue-restricted layers, investigate less regular predecessor structures, and compare the resulting statements with the literature before claiming novelty. Symmetry compression of the first factorial model yields $F_n=nF_{n-1}$, ordinary sequential multiplication; no improved factorial algorithm is established. The wider Catalan and algebraic programme remains open.
