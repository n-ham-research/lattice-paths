\section{Introduction}
Ham's 2019 collection \cite{Ham2019} records numerical relationships between lattice paths and integer sequences. East and Ham \cite{EastHam} study finiteness, geometry and algebraic structures of step-set walks. Their earlier version \cite[Example 3.14]{EastHamV1} already proves complete-triangle factorial counts, nonzero-triangle totals and alternating endpoint formulas. Rorabaugh's 2015 OEIS contribution \cite{Rorabaugh} proves the axis alternating-factorial interpretation. These are prior results, not discoveries of the present investigation.

Our structural question concerns when a translation-defined step relation preserves residue-block-constant inputs in every growing layer. The quotient-matrix principle is standard equitable-partition theory \cite[Chapter 3]{CoutinhoGodsil}; we prove an exact finite converse for triangular windows and a boundary criterion for arbitrary intervals. This is stronger than verifying one observed counting trajectory. We then apply classical plane-tree encodings \cite{HacklEtAl,BrakEtAl} to align several source models and their generating functions. Limited targeted searches have not determined priority for the finite converse; no novelty claim is made.
