\section{Introduction}
Ham's collection \cite{Ham2019} motivates structural questions beyond individual sequence identities. East and Ham \cite{EastHam} distinguish words, endpoints and constrained walks, and propose a category retaining arbitrary starting points. The motivating factorial and alternating endpoint examples are prior work \cite[Example 3.14]{EastHamV1}.

Here we ask which translation-defined transitions preserve residue-constant functions, what actual origin counts imply, and when labelled endpoint data identify jump weights. Equitable-partition invariance is standard \cite{BaileyEtAl}; the expanding-window converses require additional geometric arguments. We sharpen an obstruction using only one actual trajectory, reconstruct kernels on widening funnels, and connect compressed scalar and parity observations with inverse identification. Fixed-graph walk-matrix recovery \cite{LiuSiemons} supplies related context with different data. The exact priority of the geometry-specific converses, boundary certificate and compressed tests remains unresolved.
