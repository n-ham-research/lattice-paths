\section{Introduction}
Ham's 2019 collection \cite{Ham2019} records many numerical relationships between lattice paths and integer sequences. The earlier work of East and Ham \cite{EastHam} studies finiteness, geometry and algebraic structures of step-set walks. Its unrestricted endpoint monoid should be distinguished from walks constrained throughout to a region. The present foundation isolates elementary mechanisms behind Sections 24--27 of the collection. Numerical agreement motivates the questions but is not used as proof. In particular, Rorabaugh's 2015 comment in OEIS A005165 \cite{Rorabaugh} already proves the alternating-factorial interpretation considered below.
