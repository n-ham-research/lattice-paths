\begin{abstract}
We study what structural and observed residue regularity imply about a translation-defined jump kernel. Boundary comparisons give finite triangular rigidity and scalar rigidity on arbitrarily widening funnels. A boundary propagation argument sharpens a finite-range obstruction for actual nonnegative counting trajectories, with separate optimality statements for prescribed starting rows and origin-generated binomial models. Complete labelled rows recover observable weights on widening funnels. Nonzero scalar uniformity identifies kernel shape, while parity uniformity with equal positive initial weights gives sharp inverse horizons of six over the nonnegative reals, five over the rationals and three for indicators. Compressed observations retain reflection ambiguities and finite data cannot determine invisible tails. Historical priority and standalone publication value remain unresolved.
\end{abstract}
