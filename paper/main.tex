\begin{abstract}
We revisit lattice-path observations of Ham through finite layered graphs. Constant predecessor counts give uniform endpoint products, whereas constant successor counts give products for column totals. These distinct mechanisms explain factorial, alternating-factorial, double-factorial and factorial-product examples, with explicit support conditions and index shifts. The results here are elementary verified foundations; no claim of novelty is made. In particular, the alternating-factorial interpretation has an earlier recorded proof.
\end{abstract}
