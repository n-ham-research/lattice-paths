\begin{abstract}
We study residue compression and combinatorial encodings for restricted lattice walks. For triangular layers we prove a finite rigidity criterion: all transitions through a horizon $N\ge q$ preserve every vector constant on residues modulo $q$ if and only if the allowed-jump indicator is periodic modulo $q$ on the observable difference window. An exact boundary criterion handles general interval layers. We also give explicit classical excursion--tree--Dyck bijections with the size conventions needed to unify several earlier observations, alongside factorial examples. The classification is independently verified here; its historical priority remains unresolved. Classical bijections and previously proved factorial results are attributed separately.
\end{abstract}
