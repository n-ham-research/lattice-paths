# Working bibliography

This register distinguishes primary material actually available locally from leads requiring subsequent inspection. No claim of novelty follows from a reference's presence or absence.

1. **Nicholas Ham.** *Lattice path conjectures*. 11 May 2019. [Author-hosted PDF](https://n-ham.com/old-website/pdfs/Lattice%20Path%20Conjectures.pdf). Primary research notebook: 41 numbered sections, ten numerical endpoint arrays. Entire source read for the conjecture inventory. Contains informal and tentative matches; it is not a collection of established theorems. The PDF SHA-256 is `7cd60b318fdaed8fd9c5c3e76a56b626fa83c2dc6d94217bef0c437a6f17138a`.

2. **James East and Nicholas Ham.** *Lattice paths and submonoids of Z²*. [arXiv:1811.05735](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.05735), cached v3 dated 27 May 2021. Primary source for unrestricted/constrained path conventions and geometric/algebraic finiteness properties. The cached PDF SHA-256 is `328ccb8b369e6931091a7c99b60f6e615107c29a74c55af6bc0dafeba722879f`. Use the exact section/result references in the research literature audit; do not assume boundary restrictions agree with Ham (2019).

3. **Nicholas C. Ham.** *Lattice path enumeration algorithms*. [GitLab repository](https://gitlab.com/n-ham-paper-files/lattice-path-algorithms). Historical C++ implementation associated with earlier East–Ham algorithms, source cited in Ham (2019) as September 2018. Inspected commit `e470a327c0f9fa29f52cd9b287bfc132af7a6749`, dated 11 May 2019. Licence declaration: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International. See [algorithm audit](algorithms-audit.md) for the actual scope of reproduction and limitations.

4. **The OEIS Foundation Inc.** *The On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences*. [OEIS](https://oeis.org/). The inventory records each supplied `Axxxxxx` identifier and its claimed extraction. Identifiers are bibliographic leads until the corresponding entry, offset, formula, and references have been independently inspected. Different identifiers separated by slashes in the source are candidate matches, not proved equal sequences.

Additional primary literature discovered during investigation should be documented with the exact theorem or formula used and the scope of inspection in `research/literature-review.md`. The preexisting joint work must remain attributed to both authors even while Nicholas Ham is the sole author of the present manuscript.
