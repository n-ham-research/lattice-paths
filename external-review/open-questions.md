# Questions for mathematical discussion

1. **Recognition.** Is the support-dependent finite-range certificate a familiar consequence of positive-kernel or killed-walk theory? Does the precise expanding-window formulation add useful mathematical content?
2. **Recognition.** Are the finite triangular/funnel converses or reconstruction statements standard structured-matrix consequences? How should their relationship to equitable partitions and inverse graph problems be stated?
3. **Periodic seeds.** For an indicator kernel with 0,1 in its support, does all-prefix actual q-uniformity force q-periodicity for every q? The established seed horizons are 3 for q=2 and 4 for q=3. A positive nonperiodic q=5 prefix survives through six. The bootstrap after a periodic seed is proved; obtaining a seed in general is open.
4. **Hypotheses and horizons.** What replaces the boundary pivots or 0,1 reachability assumptions? What is the optimal origin-start finite-support certificate? The restarted-data optimum is not an answer to the latter question.
5. **Observations and geometry.** Which compressed observations identify useful kernel classes? Reflection preserves row totals/multisets but permutes labelled endpoints and residues. Finite data cannot determine invisible tails. Which geometry or additional observations remove meaningful ambiguities?
6. **Other regimes.** Eventual-only uniformity, signed kernels and sparse nonconsecutive uniform rows remain distinct open extensions. Existing nonnegative proofs do not settle them.

The broader packed-word, blob and Motzkin/Riordan questions remain in the original programme. They are not required to assess this manuscript. No direct bijection or new resolution of those families is asserted here.
