# Primary reference guide

The manuscript bibliography contains full citations. Links are supplied instead of redistributing third-party papers.

- East–Ham, *Lattice paths and submonoids of Z²*: [v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.05735v1), Example 3.14 and §4; [v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.05735v3), Lemma 3.2 and Remark 3.4. Earlier factorial examples and constrained-walk setting.
- Bailey–Cameron–Gavrilyuk–Goryainov, *Equitable partitions of Latin-square graphs*, [arXiv:1802.01001](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.01001), §2. Established invariant-space background.
- Lawler–Limic, *Random Walk: A Modern Introduction*, [author-hosted text](https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~lawler/srwbook.pdf), §§5.2 and 12.4.1. Killing and fixed-set restricted transition operators.
- Liu–Siemons, *Unlocking the Walk Matrix of a Graph*, [arXiv:1911.00062v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.00062v3), Theorems 1.2, 5.1, 5.5 and 6.1. Fixed undirected graph reconstruction and walk equivalence.
- Blåsten–Isozaki–Lassas–Lu, *Inverse problems for discrete heat equations and random walks for a class of graphs*, [arXiv:2107.00494v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.00494v2), Condition 1.4 and Theorems 1.5–1.8. Different finite-graph/access/hitting-data hypotheses.
- De la Peña–Gzyl–McDonald, *Inverse problems for random walks on trees: network tomography*, [arXiv:math/0610821v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0610821v1), Theorem 1.1. Reconstruction from hitting-time/place data on an augmented tree.

The novelty assessment records what was actually inspected and any retrieval limits. Different hypotheses do not establish originality; no unsuccessful search is treated as evidence of novelty.
