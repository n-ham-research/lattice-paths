Bootstrap uses m≥q and nonzero boundary pivots. Finite positivity or one successful prefix does not prove a general converse.

Funnel inversion needs complete compatible labelled rows and both preceding nonzero corner pivots; widths may jump or plateau. Field statements are conditional on pivots. Ordinary indicator positivity does not survive arbitrary positive-characteristic reduction. Reflection preserves weight, length, identities and composition in chronological order; it reverses endpoint labels and permutes residue/diagonal observations while preserving row totals/multisets. Reflection is not a classification of all ambiguities.
