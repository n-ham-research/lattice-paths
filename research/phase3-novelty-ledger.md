# Phase 3 targeted novelty ledger

16 October2026. This ledger separates proved mathematics from first-priority claims. No entry is certified novel. A failure to find an exact theorem is not evidence of originality. Literature coverage is bounded as specified below.

## Material directly inspected in this pass

- Bailey, Cameron, Gavrilyuk and Goryainov, *Equitable partitions of Latin-square graphs*, arXiv:1802.01001v1, §2 definition and invariant-subspace argument before Proposition2.1. https://arxiv.org/html/1802.01001v1 . Relevant full section inspected, not the entire classification paper. It treats fixed undirected graphs; our incoming rectangular setting is analogous, not an unexplained claim that its stronger spectral conclusions transfer.
- Liu and Siemons, *Unlocking the Walk Matrix of a Graph*, arXiv:1911.00062v3, introduction Theorem1.2 and §5 Theorems5.1/5.5 with the displayed reconstruction argument. https://arxiv.org/html/1911.00062v3 . Their walk data are e,Ae,...,A^(n−1)e in one fixed space. Our unknowns are two new jump differences at each growing layer, so this theorem is not directly applied. No assertion that their other results cannot imply related special cases.
- Blåsten–Isozaki–Lassas–Lu, *Inverse Problems for Discrete Heat Equations and Random Walks for a Class of Graphs*, https://doi.org/10.1137/21M1439936 : publisher abstract inspected by trajectory agent, full article not read. Do not claim a theorem-level exclusion or priority determination from this abstract.
- Novelli–Thibon, arXiv:1403.5962v3, §§2.2,2.7,5.1,5.3,(182), selected tables; precise definitions and algebra checked by multiple agents and coordinator. https://arxiv.org/html/1403.5962v3 . Do not silently use (186) as the same normalization: its direct inverse yields a different second coefficient when interpreted that way. The proof relies on (182), not on a source-typo assumption.
- East–Ham v1/v2/v3: exact relevant locations and scope in phase3-east-ham-versions.md. This is the source of old factorial and constrained-category material.

## Search questions and remaining publication blocker

Queries focused on: growing Toeplitz truncations preserving residue-constant spaces; inverse endpoint walk data and jump-set recovery; equitable residue partitions of Toeplitz graphs; finite-range killed convolution with uniform growing-domain distributions. Searches returned fixed-graph spectral/equitable work, structured-matrix papers with different settings, and inverse graph theory. No exact match was identified among inspected sources. This limited search does not certify a new result, nor justify spending weeks extending an unvetted lemma.

Recommended next comparison is a specialist assessment of the exact finite-window converse and the actual-trajectory mass obstruction, with their short proofs supplied. The mathematical briefing asks these precise questions rather than promoting them as already publishable. A direct comparison with positive-operator/killed-walk and structured-matrix literature remains open.
