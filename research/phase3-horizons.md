# Minimal horizons and geometric obstructions

16 October2026. Exact statements versus open optimization questions.

1. In the triangle, the first transition with a repeated target residue modulo q is n=q. Thus no earlier transition can place a nontrivial preservation constraint on any jump weights. At N=q the boundary constraints join f(−r)=f(q−r), 0≤r<q, exactly all neighboring residue pairs on W_q. This informative horizon is algebraically sharp, not just an artifact of the induction.

2. For q≥3, taking N=q−1 and f=1_{q−1} gives a concrete failure of the periodic-window conclusion: every transition is vacuously residue-regular, but −1 and q−1 are observable and congruent with different weights. For q=1,2 the smaller windows can have no repeated observable residue at all, so the statement 'N≥q is always the least horizon at which the equivalence is true' would be misleading. It is the first informative transition horizon; vacuous equivalences may hold earlier.

3. For integer cones of width kn, the first repeated target residue occurs at ceil(q/k). At that first horizon every source cell is singleton, and the sliding edges cover the neighboring q-pairs on the observable window. The proposed all-later-horizon q>1 cone classification remains unproved. The scalar q=1 widening-funnel theorem has exact finite windows at every N; no waiting bound is needed beyond the first transition.

4. Actual counting trajectories have a different horizon. The q=5,N=6 example with jumps{−5,−3,0,1,4,6} has positive counts at every endpoint and residue-uniform rows through6, but is nonperiodic on W_6. Rows5 and6 are respectively [6,8,10,10,8,6] and [22,22,24,20,24,22,22]. Row7=[64,90,68,66,66,68,90,64] already fails the required equality at positions0 and5. Therefore positivity does not transfer the structural finite horizon. No universal optimal actual-trajectory horizon is proved.

5. For finite nonnegative support, the at-most2q late-row certificate is sufficient, not claimed optimal. In general windows, the proved certificate is the strict gap floor(|I_H|/q)>ceil(|I_B|/q) plus full-predecessor cores for each intervening transition. This is a geometric counting condition, with an exact proof; identifying its optimal replacement is open.

6. Nonmonotone or bounded periodically varying geometry can prevent scalar rigidity indefinitely. Let I_(2j)={0}, I_(2j+1)={0,1}, and f(0)=f(1)=1,f(−1)=0. Every transition preserves constant inputs: transitions to a singleton are vacuous, and those from0 have identical weights to0,1. Yet U={−1,0,1} is not constant. Its actual trajectory is also positive and uniform in every layer. This preserves a precise obstruction to extending the widening-funnel theorem without geometric hypotheses. The general component graph remains the exact finite decision criterion.
