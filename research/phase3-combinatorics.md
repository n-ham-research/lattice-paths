## Precise profile theorem

Paths start at (0,0), have increments +1 and -a, a in a fixed subset of the nonnegative integers, and end at height zero with every visited vertex nonnegative. They are words in a set of steps, including at most one zero increment. Straight segments remain nonnegative precisely when their endpoints do. Let r_a be finitely supported nonnegative multiplicities, u=sum a*r_a, r=sum r_a, N=u+r. The empty word is included. Horizontal size is N for unit-horizontal steps and u for positive vertical downsteps; the latter construction excludes a=0 to ensure finite coordinate counts.

The number with this exact profile is

    N! / ((u+1)! product_a r_a!).

The tree bijection in phase2-bijections.md sends this profile to a plane tree with u+1 leaves and r_a vertices of degree a+1. To prove the formula independently, arrange a multiset of N+1 increments comprising u+1 copies of -1 and r_a copies of a. Their sum is -1. Among the N+1 marked starting positions of every circular word there is exactly one reading with all proper prefix sums nonnegative. Here is the elementary rotation argument: for a linear reading with prefix sums S_0=0,...,S_(N+1)=-1, rotate after the first occurrence of its global minimum (identifying position N+1 with position zero). Prefix sums after that cut are nonnegative until wrapping; before the cut all integer sums exceed the minimum by at least one, so the wrapped proper prefixes are also nonnegative. Conversely a good cut must attain the global minimum and cannot have an earlier equal minimum, by the same wrapped inequality. The period is the whole length, since any proper repetition would divide the total sum -1. Consequently every rotation orbit has N+1 distinct words. Divide its multinomial count by N+1. The good readings are exactly the preorder degree words after subtracting one from each degree. Delete the final leaf, then reverse-negate the remaining increments to recover the excursion. This proves the result, including N=0.

Under the explicit polygon duality, an internal degree d corresponds to a face of d+1 sides. Thus a nonempty excursion profile (r>=1) corresponds to exactly r_a faces of a+2 sides, whenever a>=1. Its polygon has u+2 boundary sides and r-1 diagonals, hence N+1 total edges. The empty profile instead represents the separate formal one-edge object, with no ordinary polygon or faces. For §18 specialize to positive even a. The formula therefore refines the established polygon equality by the entire face-size profile. This is the classical plane-tree degree enumeration in the project's coordinates, not a proposed new theorem.

## §23: exact primary definition and extraction

Primary reading: Novelli–Thibon, [arXiv:1403.5962v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1403.5962v3), §§2.2, 2.7, 5.1, 5.3, especially (31), (182). A packed word uses exactly an initial interval of positive letters; an m-packed word of degree n has length mn and every letter multiplicity divisible by m. Sylvester equivalence is generated by exchanging adjacent a,c when a<=b<c for some later b. Formula (182) counts its classes at x=2. If (186) is read in the same displayed polynomial normalization as (182), its inverse gives second coefficient 1+2m rather than m+2. A different peak-weight convention may explain this apparent inconsistency; (186) is not used here. Reading scope was the cited sections and supporting definitions, not the entire Hopf-algebra paper.

For the source path model S={(1,1)} union {(0,-mk):k>=1}, m>=1, consider paths in y>=0 from (0,0) to (mn,0). Every point on a segment stays in the region; empty path counted once. They have mn upsteps, boundedly many downward steps, and hence finite counts. Write H_m(t) for the generating function in n. The already proved tree decomposition gives

    H = 1 + t H^m (2H-1).

Let C_n denote the primary formula (182) evaluated at x=2. Its finite binomial sum is equivalently

    C_n = [z^n] ((1-z)/(1-2z))^(mn+1) / (mn+1).

This equivalence follows just by expanding (1-z)^(mn+1) and (1-2z)^(-mn-1); it is not a numerical identification. To compare it with H rigorously, put F(z)=(1-z)/(1-2z), and let Z=t F(Z)^m be its unique zero-constant formal solution. Lagrange inversion gives, for n>=1,

    [t^n]F(Z) = (1/n)[z^(n-1)] F'(z) F(z)^(mn)
              = [z^n]F(z)^(mn+1)/(mn+1).

Since F(Z)=H implies Z=(H-1)/(2H-1), its defining equation is exactly H-1=tH^m(2H-1). Both constant terms are 1, so C_n=[t^n]H for all n>=0. This proves the source family identification with the exact size relation: horizontal endpoint mn corresponds to word length mn and packed-word degree n. It extends the source m>=3 family to m>=1. For m=3 the initial terms are 1,1,5,34,267,2279 (including degree zero); these agree with the primary sequence after its displayed degree-one initial term. Equality of counts is proved; a direct map from path words to congruence classes has not been constructed.
