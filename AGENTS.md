# Research instructions

Human author: Nicholas Ham. Read research/handover-phase2.md, research/conjecture-audit.md, research/phase2-claim-status.json and reviews/review-log.md before new research.

- Define the step set, start, endpoint, region, vertex/segment convention, empty path and indexing before counting.
- Distinguish literature results, independently verified source observations, candidate extensions, conjectures, counterexamples and open questions. No novelty claim without literature comparison.
- Counts are words in a step-set (a set, not a multiset). Zero displacement can make counts infinite. Unit horizontal increment is a restricted engine domain, never a general assumption about the sources.
- Keep speculative results in research/. Promote only complete proofs surviving counterexample checks, source comparison and internal review.
- Use exact integers. Match DP against explicit enumeration on small domains; tests are evidence, not proofs.
- For substantive results use three initially separate review agents where practical: inference, counterexamples, generalization. Record actual identities, supplied context and limitations. Internal AI reviews are not external peer review.
- Preserve corrections and rejected claims, source versions, dates and hashes. Never copy unlicensed external code or PDFs into Git. Cache in ignored .local/.
- Run scripts/validate.sh before pushing relevant work. Inspect rendered manuscript pages.
- Nicholas Ham is sole author; James East has not agreed to this project. Do not contact him. Log actual AI contributions and do not invent human sign-off or model versions.
- Do not submit to arXiv or journals without explicit author approval. Licensing reserved pending author selection.
